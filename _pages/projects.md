I like to build personal projects for my own use. Here are the main ones, with the posts that document them.

## Home automation

**[Sprinkler system](/automation/sprinkler/)**
A garden sprinkler controlled by a Raspberry Pi. The series covers the [hydraulic part](/automation/sprinkler-hydraulic/), the [electrical part](/automation/sprinkler-elec/) and the [software part](/automation/sprinkler-software/). A later post shows how to use a [Netatmo rain sensor](/automation/sprinkler-netatmo/) to regulate it.

**[Reactive weather station](/automation/reactive-weather-station/)**
A reactive architecture built around a Raspberry Pi 3, which produces temperature and humidity data.

**Home alarm** (in progress)
A security alarm built with a Raspberry Pi. Read the [project post](/project/alarming-system/).
